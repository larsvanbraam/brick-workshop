# Rebrickable part vocabulary by topic

Generated by `pnpm rebrickable:vocabulary` from the Rebrickable bulk downloads (retrieved 2026-11-23). Sets of 150+ pieces from 2000 on; spares, figures, stickers and electrics are left out. Inventories list parts, not positions: this is the vocabulary official designers reach for, not how they place it. The technique notes next to this file cover placement.

_Coverage_ is the share of the topic's sets that contain the design; _lift_ is that share divided by the share across all sets (2.0 = twice as common here). _Engine_ says whether the catalogue can place the design today; a "no" on a high-lift part is a candidate for `docs`/README's "adding a part".
